# Shurer Dhara New York

Shurer Dhara NY began its journey in June 2019 as an in-person platform. Classes were held twice a month in Jackson Heights, New York. The program started with 8–10 students and a vision for growth that was reflected in every step of its development.

Unfortunately, circumstances changed when the COVID-19 pandemic struck. The platform transitioned to an online format. While this shift was initially challenging, the outcome proved to be highly positive. In June 2020, online classes were officially launched, enabling students from the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Turkey to connect and learn together. Many of the participants are Shurer Dhara Dhaka alumni from the 1990s. Today, the platform has around 35 students, and classes are held every Sunday morning (New York time).

As a group, Shurer Dhara NY has presented two major productions to date. Kanikanjali was staged in New Jersey in August 2024, and Shyama, the dance drama, was performed in New York in October 2025. In addition to these major productions, Shurer Dhara NY has participated in numerous cultural events organized by various community organizations.

Shurer Dhara NY has also demonstrated a strong commitment to supporting the artistic community during times of need. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the organization hosted an online fundraiser, the COVID-19 Benefit Concert, to raise funds for artists and musicians who were adversely affected by the pandemic.

What is particularly remarkable is that although students meet and learn online throughout the year, they travel from different cities and countries to come together for major performances. These gatherings serve not only as artistic collaborations but also as reunions, united by a shared love of music and the enduring bond of the Shurer Dhara family.

Shurer Dhara NY is dedicated to preserving the traditional Guru-Shishya Parampara and ensuring that this rich musical heritage is passed down to future generations, particularly second-generation Bengalis growing up outside the sub continent. Through its commitment to excellence in music education and cultural continuity, the platform seeks to nurture a lifelong appreciation for Bengali music, culture, and values.

The Shurer Dhara NY online platform is coordinated by Bidisha, Elma, Priyodorshini, and Shariar, all of whom are Shurer Dhara Dhaka alumni and have been part of the core Shurer Dhara family since its inception.

## Notable Productions

### Kanikanjali

A tribute woven with reverence and remembrance, Kanikanjali marked the birth centenary of the legendary Tagore Singer Kanika Bandyopadhyay, whose guidance helped shape the early musical journey of Prof. (Dr.) Rezwana Choudhury Bannya.

### Shyama

Presented by Shurer Dhara New York in October 2025, Shyama brought to life one of Rabindranath Tagore's most powerful tales of love, sacrifice, and redemption.

### COVID-19 Fundraiser (2020)

In response to the challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic, Shurer Dhara New York organized an online Benefit Concert to support artists and musicians affected by the crisis.
