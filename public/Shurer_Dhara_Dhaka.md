# Shurer Dhara Dhaka

The first school of SD, founded in 1992, with a few students in a small room of her residence, now running with thousands of students from all over the country.

The Institute runs a heritage music school in Lalmatia, Dhaka, through a distinct and approved curriculum in online and offline mode.

Has successfully completed its Silver Jubilee with a gala event and dignitaries participating across the continents.
