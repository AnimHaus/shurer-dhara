# Shurer Dhara Toronto

From its roots in Dhaka to communities across the world, the journey of Shurer Dhara has now found a new home in Toronto. Launched on 19 April 2026 by five proud alumni of Shurer Dhara, the Toronto chapter carries forward the music, values, and traditions that shaped their own artistic journeys. Gathering every Sunday in Scarborough, children and adults come together to learn, sing, and celebrate the timeless legacy of Rabindranath Tagore, extending Shurer Dhara's melody to yet another corner of the world.

## Shurer Dhara Toronto Team (Teachers)

The Shurer Dhara Toronto team is led by former students of Shurer Dhara Dhaka, who now carry forward the musical tradition that shaped their own journeys. Bound by a shared love for Tagore's music and the teachings of Prof. (Dr.) Rezwana Choudhury Bannya, they continue to nurture and inspire a new generation of learners in Canada.
